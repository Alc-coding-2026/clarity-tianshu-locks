## s.750(2) undertaking / LOS route (not GC letter)

### English

```
Note: In connection with the Company's cessation of business and planned
deregistration, the directors have confirmed that all outstanding assets
and liabilities, including accrued expenses of HK$[X,XXX], amounts due to
directors of HK$[X,XXX] and amounts due to shareholders of HK$[X,XXX],
will be settled or formally waived prior to the submission of the
deregistration application to the Companies Registry, as required under
Section 750(2) of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance.
```

### 中文

```
關於本公司停止業務營運及擬申請撤銷登記，董事已確認所有未償還資產及
負債，包括應計費用[X]港元、應付董事款項[X]港元及應付股東款項[X]港元，
將於根據《香港公司條例》（第622章）第750(2)條向公司註冊處提交撤銷
登記申請前悉數結清或正式豁免。
```
