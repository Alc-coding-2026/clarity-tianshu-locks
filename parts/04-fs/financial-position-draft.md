## LOCKED — Cap. 622 s.387 SoFP signing block / 財務狀況表簽署欄

**Status: LOCKED — Allen 2026-10-17** (corrected to house faces: separate “Date of approval:” line). EN + editorial 繁體中文. Chinese is **not** an official Companies Registry / HKICPA translation.

**Statute:** Cap. 622 s.387 — the statement of financial position that forms part of the financial statements must be approved by the directors and signed on the directors' behalf by **2 directors**, or (where the company has only one director) by **that director**. s.387(2) requires the **names** of the persons who signed to be stated.

**House face sources (approved packs):**
- Two directors: Budsilver YE2024 / YE2025 — `/workspace/approved-docx-amend-refs/Budsilver_FS_20240331_incorp-period.docx`, `Budsilver_FS_20250331_YE.docx`
- One director: QCAC YE2026 — `/workspace/approved-docx-amend-refs/QCAC_FS_20260331_YE_NL-no-revenue-1dir.docx`

**Rules**
- Place this block **immediately under** the SoFP totals (after Total equity / 權益總額), before the notes.
- Choose **Path 2D** or **Path 1D** from the company register at the approval date — do not invent a second director.
- State the **signing directors' names** under the signature lines (s.387(2)). Title line under each name is **Director** / **董事**.
- House live faces keep the same approval lead-in for one-director and two-director companies ("Board of Directors" / "Signed on behalf of the Board by"). Do **not** retitle the block "Sole director" in the caption.
- House faces carry a separate **Date of approval: / 批准日期：** line under the approval sentence (QCAC, Budsilver, Haoyi, Uwin, HKDRC). Do not put the date inside the sentence; align the pack's AR / DR report date with the actual board approval date. Optional Path D (date in the approval sentence) is offered below if Allen wants HKAS 10.17-style dating on the face.
- Only a director in office at the approval / signing date may sign.
- Framework name in the FS notes stays on the **QCAC house form** until Allen's separate framework lock — not reinvented here.
