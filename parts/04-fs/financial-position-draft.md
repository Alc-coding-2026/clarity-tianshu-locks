**Face rules (firm lock)**
- Receivables / payables captions: **Accounts and other receivables** / **Accounts and other payables** (not Trade and other…).
- **No** amounts due from/to directors on the face — detail in Note 11 (s.383(1)(d)) + Notes 13/15 aggregate lines.
- **Income tax recoverable** and **Income tax payable** shown separately on the face.
- **Bank loans** shown separately as **current** and **non-current**.
- Equity holdings on face (non-current), when present: **Investments in subsidiaries**, **Investments in associates**, **Investments in joint ventures**, and **Equity investments** (other equity holdings). Amounts **due from/to** those parties stay in Notes 13/15 only.
- Remove unused lines in a real pack; keep placeholders here for the specimen.
- Section-heading rows take no amounts or note numbers.
