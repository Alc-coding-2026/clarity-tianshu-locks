    **EN.** Service-cost illustration with two annual columns. Replace every amount and note reference; use parentheses consistently for expenses/losses in the completed statement. Retain applicable lines and add material categories required by the entity’s facts. Reconcile gross profit, profit before tax and profit for the year. A trading entity needs the appropriate cost-of-sales wording; loss labels, first reporting periods, unaudited comparatives and restatements need separate review. Zero-filled workbook cells are not specimen financial results.

    **中文。** 本例以服務成本及兩個年度欄呈列。替換所有金額及附註編號；完成報表時一致使用括號表示費用／虧損。保留適用項目，並按實際情況加入重要類別。核對毛利、除稅前溢利及本年度溢利。貿易公司須採用恰當的銷售成本措辭；虧損標題、首個報告期間、未經審計比較數字及重列須另行覆核。工作簿中的零數字並非範例財務結果。
