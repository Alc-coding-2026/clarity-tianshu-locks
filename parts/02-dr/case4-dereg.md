# Directors' Report — Case 4 Deregistration / cessation / 董事報告（結業／撤銷登記）

**Allen lock 2026-09-19.** EN + 繁體中文.

**Rules**
- Period ends on **cessation date**, labelled “(date of cessation of business)” / “（結業日期）”.
- **First-period from incorporation:** use Opening A (首份 / from incorporation).
- **Mid-life cessation:** use Opening B — actual period start; do **not** say “first report” / from incorporation unless facts match.
- Directors: list names only; **do not invent** appoint dates; **do not** add “no director was appointed during the period.”
- Auditor: **omit** AGM / further-appointment resolution; keep only “will not be proposed.”
- Break-up detail stays in **Note 2** — DR only cross-refers.

---

## Opening A — first period (incorporation → cessation)

### English

```
DIRECTORS' REPORT

The directors of [COMPANY NAME] (the "Company") present their first
report and the audited financial statements of the Company for the
period from [INCORPORATION DATE] (date of incorporation) to
[CESSATION DATE] (date of cessation of business).
```

### 中文

```
董事報告

[公司名稱]（「本公司」）董事提呈其首份董事報告及本公司由[成立日期]
（成立日期）至[結業日期]（結業日期）之經審核財務報表。
```

## State of affairs

### English

```
STATE OF AFFAIRS

The Company ceased business operations on [CESSATION DATE] and the
directors intend to apply for deregistration with the Companies Registry.
The financial statements have been prepared on a break-up basis as
described in Note 2 to the financial statements.
```

### 中文

```
業務狀況

本公司於[結業日期]停止業務運營，董事擬向公司註冊處申請撤銷登記。
財務報表已按變現基礎編製，詳情載於財務報表附註2。
```

## Directors

### English

```
The directors of the Company who held office during the period and up
to the date of this report were:-

[DIRECTOR NAME(S)]

Given the Company's cessation of business and planned deregistration,
there will be no future appointments of directors.
```

### 中文

```
於本期間及截至本報告日期止任職之本公司董事為：-

[董事姓名]

基於本公司已停止業務並擬申請撤銷登記，將不會再委任董事。
```

## Auditor

### English

```
The financial statements have been audited by [Firm]. Given the
Company's planned deregistration, no resolution for the reappointment
of auditors will be proposed.

This represents the final Directors' Report of the Company in
preparation for its deregistration.
```

### 中文

```
本財務報表已由[事務所]審核。由於本公司擬申請撤銷登記，將不會提呈
續聘核數師之決議案。

本董事報告為本公司就擬申請撤銷登記而編製之最後一份董事報告。
```
