??? note "使用及來源 / Use and provenance"

    **EN.** An original assembly of report-information fields, not a statutory contents checklist. Match names, dates and currency to the final report. The corporate-information block is optional presentation material; check whether other details are needed. Use only after confirming Hong Kong incorporation and the stated profile. For a non-annual period, obtain the correct approved period heading. No framework-compliance or audit-opinion sentence is supplied here.

    **中文。** 本頁按報告資料欄位編排，並非法定披露清單。名稱、日期及貨幣須與最終報告一致。公司資料區塊屬可選的呈列資料；另行核實是否需要其他資料。僅供已確認香港註冊及上述分類之個案使用。非年度期間須另取恰當的已批准期間標題。本頁不提供準則合規或審計意見文句。

    **Source.** User-supplied `afs_template.xlsm`, version `26.8.1`: `1 - REPORT INFO!B19:F22` (currency and names), `B27:F34` (incorporation, registered office and reporting dates), `B43:F44` (signers); `3 - DIRECTORS LIST!A1:I2` (director-name fields only). The auditor-name field and page composition are editorial additions. Workbook example names and dates are replaced by placeholders.

    **Technical reference.** The firm’s local HKICPA SME-FRF & SME-FRS (March 2025), paragraph 1.12 (printed pages 22–23; PDF pages 26–27), supports identifying the reporting entity, date/period and reporting currency. Checked from full text on 13 September 2026. This does not prescribe the optional corporate-information block. [HKICPA standard reference](https://www.hkicpa.org.hk/-/media/HKICPA-Website/Members-Handbook/volumeII/smefrf1221.pdf) (public download currently redirects to 404).

    **技術依據。** 已按2025年3月版本第1.12段全文核對報告主體、日期／期間及呈列貨幣之識別要求；該段並非規定本頁可選的公司資料區塊。
