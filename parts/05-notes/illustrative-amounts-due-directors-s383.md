# Note 11 — Amounts Due from/(to) the Directors (Cap.622 s.383(1)(d))

**Allen lock 2026-09-14** (base + Variants A and C) — **non-loan path** (year-end only). Working bible specimen.

**Status 2026-10-10: DRAFT — pending Allen lock** for the **non-loan vs loan/quasi-loan path split** and for **Path L** (full Cap.622G form) below. Base EN/ZH and Variants A/C remain the locked non-loan wording until Allen locks the split.

**Rules**
- Separate statutory note under **section 383(1)(d)** of the Companies Ordinance.
- Heading always **plural**: Amounts Due from/(to) the Directors / 應收／（應付）董事款項 — even if only one director.
- Aggregate total must **tie** to the directors line in Note 13 (from) / Note 15 (to).
- SoFP face does **not** show a separate directors line — only Accounts and other receivables / payables.
- Directors appointed **before** year end are simply named in the table — **no** extra change-of-directorship paragraph (Variant B not used).

### Path split (DRAFT — pending Allen lock 2026-10-10)

Allen instruction (Clarity Quality room, 2026-10-10): Cap.622G Part 3 / s.15 applies only when the balance is a **loan, quasi-loan or credit transaction** (or related guarantee/security). Otherwise the locked year-end s.383(1)(d) note is enough.

| Path | When | What to use |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **NL — Non-loan** (locked) | Balance is **not** a loan / quasi-loan / credit transaction — e.g. unpaid share capital, expenses paid by the director on the company's behalf, ordinary current account / working-capital float with no loan terms | **Base** (year-end CY/PY only) + Variants A/C if applicable. **Do not** add highest-in-year, overdue, provisions, or guarantee columns. Name **directors as at the year-end date** only. |
| **L — Loan / quasi-loan** (DRAFT) | Balance **is** a loan, quasi-loan or credit transaction in favour of a director (or related guarantee/security under Cap.622G s.15(3)(c)) | **Path L** fuller form below (Cap.622G s.15(3) / s.16). |

**Dormant default:** On dormants the amount due from/(to) the director is usually unpaid capital or expenses the director paid — **not** a loan. Use **Path NL** (year-end note only) unless the engagement file shows clear loan / quasi-loan terms.

## Variant A — Post-YE succession (Allen lock 2026-10-17, supersedes 2026-09-14)

**When:** All directors as at year end resigned, and new director(s) were appointed **after** the reporting date. Table still names the **YE-end** directors. **No dates** in this paragraph.

Append after the base unsecured/interest-free sentence:

### English

```
Subsequent to the reporting date, all the then directors resigned and
[NEW DIRECTOR NAME(S)] [was/were] appointed as director(s) of the
Company. The newly appointed director(s) [has/have] accepted the
outstanding balance of the amounts due from/(to) the directors and
undertake(s) not to demand repayment of the amounts due to the
directors until the Company is financially capable to do so.
```

Use "due from" / "due to" to match the balance direction. If the balance is **due from** directors, omit or adapt the "not demand repayment" clause.

### 中文

```
於報告日期後，當時在任之全體董事已辭任，並委任[新董事姓名]為本公司
董事。新委任董事已接納應收／（應付）董事款項之結餘，並承諾在本公司
具備財務能力償還前，不會要求償還應付董事款項。
```

## Variant C — Deregistration / cessation (Allen lock 2026-09-14)

**When:** Company ceased business / planned deregistration (break-up basis). Table still names YE-end directors.

Append after the base unsecured/interest-free sentence (may be combined with Variant A if both apply):

### English

```
Given the Company's cessation of business and planned deregistration,
the directors have confirmed that the amounts due from/(to) directors
will be settled or formally waived prior to the submission of the
deregistration application to the Companies Registry, as required under
Section 750(2) of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance.
```

Align from/to with the balance. Cross-refer the s.750(2) undertaking in Note 2 / SoFP note where used.

Optional (aligns with Case 4 DR):

```
There will be no future appointments of directors.
```

### 中文

```
基於本公司已停止業務並擬申請撤銷登記，董事已確認應收／（應付）
董事款項將於根據《香港公司條例》第750(2)條向公司註冊處提交撤銷
登記申請前悉數結清或正式豁免。
```

Optional:

```
將不會再委任董事。
```

## Path L — Loan / quasi-loan / credit transaction (Allen lock 2026-10-10 — pending Allen lock)

**When:** The amount due from/(to) a director **is** a loan, quasi-loan or credit transaction (Cap.622G s.15), or the company has given a related guarantee or security (s.15(3)(c)). Do **not** use Path L for unpaid capital or director-paid expenses on dormants.

**Scope vs Path NL**
- Name persons who were directors **at any time during the financial year** (not only at year end), including anyone who left before YE.
- Disclose Cap.622G s.15(3) particulars: principal terms; outstanding at beginning and end of year; **greatest amount outstanding during the year** (if balances varied); amounts fallen due but unpaid; provisions for non-repayment; and, where applicable, guarantees/security (max liability at beginning, end and greatest during the year; amounts paid/liabilities incurred).
- Holding-company directors and controlled bodies corporate: add rows only when the engagement has those parties (most Clarity SME/dormant packs will not).

**Optional aggregate:** Cap.622G s.16 allows a statement in lieu for quasi-loans and credit transactions in aggregate — use only when Allen agrees for that file; default below is per-director.
