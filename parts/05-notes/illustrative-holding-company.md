# Note 19 — Immediate and ultimate holding company / 直接及最終控股公司

**Allen lock 2026-09-19.** EN + 繁體中文 · scenarios S1–S4 + jurisdiction list.

**Rules**
- Delete the entire note if the Company has **no** holding company (standalone).
- Place of incorporation = **jurisdiction only** (never a city). Use the jurisdiction table below.
- Use **It** / **該公司** / **Neither of the above companies** — do **not** repeat the entity name within the same note.
- SME-FRS §16.3 minimum: name of parent + ultimate controlling party (as applicable).
- Pick **one** scenario. Public-FS vs no-public-FS is a last-sentence swap on S1/S3/S4 as needed.

---

## S1 — Immediate = Ultimate, no public FS *(default)*

### English

```
IMMEDIATE AND ULTIMATE HOLDING COMPANY

At [YE DATE], the immediate and ultimate holding company of the
Company is [NAME], a company incorporated in [JURISDICTION]. It does
not produce financial statements available for public use.
```

### 中文

```
直接及最終控股公司

於[年結日]，本公司之直接控股公司及最終控股公司均為[名稱]（於
[司法管轄區]註冊成立／成立），並無編製供公眾使用之財務報表。
```

---

## S2 — Immediate = Ultimate, has public FS

Same as S1, but replace the last sentence:

**EN:** `It produces consolidated financial statements available for public use.`

**中文:** `編製供公眾使用之合併財務報表。`

---

## S3 — Immediate ≠ Ultimate

### English

```
IMMEDIATE AND ULTIMATE HOLDING COMPANY

The immediate holding company of the Company is [IMMCO], a company
incorporated in [JURISDICTION]. The ultimate controlling party is
[ULTCO], a company incorporated in [JURISDICTION]. Neither of the
above companies produces financial statements available for public use.
```

### 中文

```
直接及最終控股公司

本公司之直接控股公司為[直接控股公司名稱]（於[司法管轄區]註冊成立／
成立）。最終控制方為[最終控制方名稱]（於[司法管轄區]註冊成立／
成立）。上述公司均無編製供公眾使用之財務報表。
```

(If either produces public FS, replace the last sentence for that fact — do not assert “neither” without verification.)

---

## S4 — Only ultimate known

### English

```
ULTIMATE HOLDING COMPANY

The ultimate holding company of the Company is [NAME], a company
incorporated in [JURISDICTION]. It does not produce financial
statements available for public use.
```

### 中文

```
最終控股公司
```
