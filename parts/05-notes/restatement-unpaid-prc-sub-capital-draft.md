> **Status: LOCKED — Allen 2026-10-22.** Case 2. Approval: [`review/approvals/2026-10-22-restatement-cases.json`](../../review/approvals/2026-10-22-restatement-cases.json). **Not** on AutoFS faces. **No** public-locks republish until MERGE.

> **Case:** at the prior year-end a **present obligation** existed to inject unpaid registered capital into a subsidiary incorporated in the **People’s Republic of China**. Cash had **not** yet been paid. Restatement is **Dr Investment in subsidiaries / Cr Amount due to subsidiary**, equal and opposite. Profit or loss and cash flows of the prior period do **not** change.
> **Generic shell:** [`wiki/docs/05-notes/restatement.md`](../../wiki/docs/05-notes/restatement.md). This file is the case-specific invent. Do **not** replace the shell’s locked 恆美麗 closing line with this note, and do **not** shorten this note to that 恆美麗 sentence.
> **Chinese:** editorial 繁體中文, not an official HKICPA translation.

**Rationale (one line):**

- **EN.** Comparative figures restated to recognise an unpaid capital contribution to a PRC subsidiary that was a present obligation at the prior year-end, by increasing investment in subsidiaries and the amount due to that subsidiary by the same amount.
- **ZH.** 比較數字已重列，以確認於上年度結日已存在現時義務之未繳中國附屬公司註冊資本出資，同額增加於附屬公司之投資及應付該附屬公司款項。

---

## When to use

Use when **all** of the following are true:

- The subsidiary is incorporated in the **People’s Republic of China** (jurisdiction only — never a city).
- At **[PY DATE]** a **present obligation** to inject the unpaid registered capital already existed.
- The contribution had **not** been paid in cash by that date, and it had **not** been recognised in the prior-year financial statements.
- The correction under **SME-FRS Section 7** is a balance-sheet gross-up only: **debit** investment in subsidiaries, **credit** amount due to the subsidiary, **equal and opposite**.
- Net assets, accumulated profits/(losses) and total equity **do not** change. There is **no** profit or loss effect and **no** prior-period cash flow.

## Do not use

- **Intention only** (no present obligation at the prior year-end). Do **not** restate. Disclose under the locked capital-commitments module instead: [CC-A / CC-N](./illustrative-commitments-events.md) (`Contracted but not provided for` / `Authorised but not contracted for`, only when that module’s facts are met).
- Cash already paid in the prior period (then the investment should already have been recognised; this gross-up is the wrong entry).
- A non-PRC subsidiary, unless Allen extends this specimen. The wording below names the People’s Republic of China.
- Sales / cost of sales misclassification — [`restatement-sales-cos-misclass-draft.md`](./restatement-sales-cos-misclass-draft.md).
- **Case 3** — [`restatement-dues-wht-overdividend-draft.md`](./restatement-dues-wht-overdividend-draft.md).
- The 恆美麗 share-capital omission.

## House rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Note number | **No fixed number.** `[X]` = next sequential note in that FS. |
| Spelling | `(Restated)` — **not** `(Restate)`. Space before the bracket. |
| Entry | Dr Investment in subsidiaries `[AMOUNT]`. Cr Amount due to subsidiary `[AMOUNT]`. The two restatement amounts are equal and opposite. |
| Where the credit sits | **Notes 13/15**, inline line `Amount due to subsidiary: [Name]` / `應付附屬公司款項：[名稱]`. **Not** inside INV-S. INV-S carries the investment only (cost less accumulated impairment). Do not park the payable on the investment note. |
| Net assets | Restatement column is nil. Accumulated profits/(losses) and total equity restatement columns are nil. `Restated` equals `As previously reported` for those three rows. |
| Closing line | Use **both** sentences in the copy block. This is **not** the 恆美麗 sentence (“no effect on the **loss** …”). This closing says **profit/(loss)** and adds the later cash settlement on the contribution schedule. |
| Cash | Prior-period cash flows are unaffected because the cash was not paid. Do not invent a cash-flow face. Later years follow the contribution schedule when cash is paid: Dr amount due to subsidiary / Cr cash. That later settlement is not this restatement. |
| Jurisdiction | `People's Republic of China` / `中華人民共和國`. Never a city. |
| Face captions | Investment line on the locked statement of financial position is `Investments in subsidiaries` / `於附屬公司之投資`. This note uses Allen’s row `Investment in subsidiaries`. Do not rename the locked face here. |

## English

```
[X]. Prior Year Restatement

During the current year, it was identified that the Company's unpaid capital
contribution to a subsidiary incorporated in the People's Republic of China
had not been recognised in the prior year financial statements. Although the
contribution had not yet been paid in cash, a present obligation to inject the
registered capital existed as at [PY DATE]. In accordance with Section 7 of the
Small and Medium-sized Entity Financial Reporting Standard, the prior year
comparative figures have been restated to recognise the investment in the
subsidiary and the corresponding amount due to the subsidiary.

The effect of the restatement on the statement of financial position as at
[PY DATE] is as follows:

|                                | As previously reported | Restatement | Restated |
|:-------------------------------|:----------------------:|:-----------:|:--------:|
| Investment in subsidiaries     | [AMOUNT]               | [AMOUNT]    | [AMOUNT] |
| Amount due to subsidiary       | [AMOUNT]               | [AMOUNT]    | [AMOUNT] |
| Net assets                     | [AMOUNT]               | —           | [AMOUNT] |
| Accumulated profits/(losses)   | [AMOUNT]               | —           | [AMOUNT] |
| Total equity                   | [AMOUNT]               | —           | [AMOUNT] |

The restatement has no effect on the profit/(loss) for the prior period or on
cash flows. Cash settlement of the unpaid capital contribution will be made in
accordance with the contribution schedule over the coming years.
```

The investment restatement and the amount-due restatement are equal and opposite. Show the payable in parentheses if that is how the face shows liabilities. Net assets, accumulated profits/(losses) and total equity do not move (`—` in the restatement column).

## Mandatory checklist — `(Restated)`

Spelling is `(Restated)`, never `(Restate)`.

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Statement of financial position — prior-year column | Add `(Restated)`. Investment and amount due to subsidiary changed, even though net assets did not. |
| Investment note (INV-S) | Add `(Restated)` on the prior-year column. Balance is the grossed-up cost **after** correction. Do **not** put the payable in this note. |
| Notes 13/15 | Add `(Restated)` on the prior-year column of the payables note if the amount-due-to-subsidiary line changed. Caption remains `Amount due to subsidiary: [Name]`. |
| Statement of profit or loss | **No** `(Restated)` — profit or loss did not change. |
| SOCIE prior-year closing | **No** `(Restated)` when accumulated profits/(losses) and total equity are unchanged. Do not label an unchanged closing. |
| Notes whose prior-year figures did not change | No `(Restated)` label. |
| Share-issuance line | **Do not add** one for this pattern. |
