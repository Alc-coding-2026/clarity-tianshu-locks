# Note 2 — Break-up basis (deregistration / cessation) / 變現基礎（撤銷登記／結業）

**Allen lock 2026-09-19.** EN + 繁體中文 · short 天書 form.

**Rules**
- Use when the Company **ceased business** and directors **intend to apply for deregistration** under Cap.622 **s.750**.
- SoFP face must carry the break-up caption (see below).
- Break-up basis is explained **only here** — AR has **no** dereg EOM / no going-concern reference.
- Excel **long** Note 2 paragraph (recoverable amounts / contingent A/L / “no material adjustments”) is **not** locked here.
- s.750(2) settle/waive undertaking is separate (SoFP footnote / Note 11 Variant C).

## Note 2 — Basis of preparation

### English

```
2. BASIS OF PREPARATION

(a) The financial statements have been prepared on a break-up basis as
the Company ceased business operations on [CESSATION DATE] and the
directors intend to apply for deregistration with the Companies Registry
pursuant to Section 750 of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance.

Under the break-up basis, assets are stated at their estimated net
realisable values and liabilities are stated at the amounts expected to
be settled. All assets and liabilities are classified as current.
```

### 中文

```
2. 編製基準

(a) 由於本公司於[結業日期]停止業務營運，且董事擬根據《香港公司條例》
第750條向公司註冊處申請撤銷登記，本財務報表已按變現基礎編製。

在變現基礎下，資產按其估計可變現淨值列賬，負債按預期清償金額列賬。
所有資產及負債均分類為流動項目。
```
