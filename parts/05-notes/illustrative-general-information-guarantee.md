**適用 / Scope:** Hong Kong company limited by guarantee (no share capital); company-level; no subsidiaries. Not for a company limited by shares. 本範例適用於香港擔保有限公司（並無股本），公司層面，且並無附屬公司。不適用於股份有限公司。股份有限公司改用 [illustrative-general-information.md](./illustrative-general-information.md)。

=== "English"

    ```text
    [1] GENERAL INFORMATION

    [Company name] (the “Company”) is incorporated and domiciled in Hong Kong and is a company limited by guarantee and not having a share capital. Its registered office is at [registered office address]. Its principal place of business is at [business address].

    The Company’s principal activity is [specific principal activity].

    In the event of the Company being wound up, every member undertakes to contribute to the assets of the Company an amount not exceeding HK$[X] per member.

    These financial statements cover the period from [period start] to [period end] and are presented in [presentation currency].
    ```

??? note "使用及來源 / Application and sources"

    Fill [X] from the articles (members’ guarantee amount). Keep the defined term as (the “Company”) / 「本公司」. Do not use Association or Society as the default. If the business address is the registered office, combine the two address sentences. Verify incorporation, domicile, legal form, addresses, actual activity and the reporting period.

    Do not add a Share Capital note for a company limited by guarantee. Do not invent a separate Members’ Guarantee note; the members’ liability line stays in this Note 1. Framework (SME-FRS vs HKFRS-PE) is out of scope of this page — Note 1 legal form only. Reporting-exemption wording for a small guarantee company under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) section 359(1)(a) belongs in Note 2 / the basis of preparation, not on this page. This page does not supply that Note 2 wording.

    以章程填寫[X]（成員擔保額）。界定詞保持（「本公司」）／(the “Company”)。不得以協會或學會作為預設界定詞。如營業地址與註冊辦事處相同，可合併兩句地址。核實註冊地、註冊所在地、法律形式、地址、實際業務及報告期間。

    擔保有限公司不得另加股本附註。不得另設獨立的「成員擔保」附註；成員責任文句留在本附註 1。準則（SME-FRS 與 HKFRS-PE）不屬本頁範圍——本頁只鎖定附註 1 的法律形式。小型擔保公司根據《公司條例》（第 622 章）第 359(1)(a) 條的報告豁免措辭屬於附註 2／編製基準，不屬於本頁。本頁不提供該附註 2 文句。
