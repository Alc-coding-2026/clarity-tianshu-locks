**Rationale (one line):**

- **EN.** Comparative figures restated to correct a prior-period error whereby amounts relating to a loan from the holding company and an investment in subsidiaries were incorrectly included in sales and cost of sales.
- **ZH.** 比較數字已重列，以更正前期錯誤，即與控股公司貸款及於附屬公司之投資有關之金額錯誤地計入銷售額及銷售成本。

---

## When to use

Use when **all** of the following are true:

- In the prior-year financial statements, amounts were included in **sales** and **cost of sales** that should have been recognised as a **loan from the holding company / amount due to holding company** and an **investment in subsidiaries**.
- The misstatement is a **prior-period error** found in the current year.
- Comparatives are corrected **retrospectively** under **SME-FRS Section 7**.
- The correction **changes profit or loss** for the prior period (the sales effect and the cost-of-sales effect need not be equal).

## Do not use

- **恆美麗 share-capital omission** (shares issued in a prior period left unrecorded; loss unchanged). Stay on the wiki shell and keep that case’s locked closing line. Do **not** use this draft’s profit/(loss) closing there.
- Change in accounting **estimate** (prospective; no restatement note).
- Voluntary change of **accounting policy** (separate Section 7 policy-change disclosure).
- A current-year loan or investment that was **never** recorded in prior-year sales or cost of sales.
- **Unpaid PRC subsidiary capital** gross-up (equal and opposite; no profit or loss effect) — [`restatement-unpaid-prc-sub-capital-draft.md`](./restatement-unpaid-prc-sub-capital-draft.md).
- **Case 3** — amounts due to shareholders and withholding tax reversed to nil (over-dividend against a negative reserve) — [`restatement-dues-wht-overdividend-draft.md`](./restatement-dues-wht-overdividend-draft.md).
- As a substitute for the locked investment note or the locked amount-due line. Those notes show the **balances after correction**. They do not replace this restatement note.

## House rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Note number | **No fixed number.** `[X]` = next sequential note in that FS. |
| Spelling | `(Restated)` — **not** `(Restate)`. Space before the bracket. |
| Liability caption | Pick **one**: `Loan from holding company` **or** `Amount due to holding company`. Delete the other before issue. Do not print the slash. Match the caption to the line that carries the corrected balance. House default for a balance folded into accounts and other payables is `Amount due to holding company: [Name]` (Notes 13/15). Use `Loan from holding company` only when that is the line actually presented. |
| Sales / cost of sales | These captions are the misclassified pair in **this** note. Do not rename the locked service income-statement specimen (`Revenue` / `Cost of services rendered`). A trading face uses sales and cost of sales. |
| Equity caption on the face | This note’s row is `Accumulated profits/(losses)`. Map it to the locked face caption actually used (`Retained earnings` or accumulated losses). Do not rename the locked face in this invent. |
| Closing line | Use the fill-in below. **Do not** use the wiki shell’s 恆美麗 sentence “The restatement has no effect on the loss for the prior period or on cash flows.” That sentence does not apply when profit or loss was affected. |
| Cash-flow sentence | Keep **one**. `There was no effect on cash flows.` when no cash-flow statement is presented (common SME pack: cash-flow statement is out of pack) or when cash totals are unchanged and nothing is reclassified. `Cash flows have been reclassified accordingly.` only when a cash-flow statement is presented and operating lines move to investing or financing. Do not invent a cash-flow face. |
| Extra rows | Do not add lines (including income tax) unless Allen extends this specimen. |
| Signs | `Restated = As previously reported + Restatement`. Parentheses for decreases, credits and losses, consistent with the face. A loan / amount due that had been credited to sales: **decrease sales**, **increase the liability**. An investment cost that had been debited to cost of sales: **decrease cost of sales**, **increase the investment**. Profit/(loss), accumulated profits/(losses) and total equity move by the **net** of those two. No share-capital movement and **no** SOCIE share-issuance line for this pattern. |
| `HK$[AMOUNT]` | Absolute amount. Choose increased **or** decreased to match the sign. Do not also put a minus inside the amount. |

## Mandatory checklist

When this note is used, do **all** of the following. Spelling is `(Restated)`, never `(Restate)`.

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Statement of profit or loss — prior-year column | Add `(Restated)`. This pattern changes sales, cost of sales and profit/(loss). |
| Statement of financial position — prior-year column | Add `(Restated)`. |
| SOCIE prior-year closing row | `Balance as at [PY DATE] (Restated)`. Space before the bracket. Use the locked row text `Balance as at`. The comparative profit/(loss) line is the **restated** amount. Current-year opening equals that restated closing. |
| Notes whose prior-year figures changed | Add `(Restated)` under the prior-year column header. Typical: sales/revenue note; investment-in-subsidiaries note; the payables note line that carries the holding-company balance. |
| Notes whose prior-year figures did not change | No `(Restated)` label. |
| Share-issuance line | **Do not add** one for this pattern. |
