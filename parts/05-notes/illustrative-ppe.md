??? note "使用及來源 / Application and sources"

    Tailor classes, useful lives, residual values and method to the asset register; the workbook’s 20% rate is only an example. Check opening + additions − disposals = closing cost; perform the corresponding accumulated-depreciation reconciliation; cost − accumulated depreciation/impairment = carrying amount. Tie closing carrying amounts to the financial position statement and the depreciation charge to expenses. Expand the schedule for impairment, reversals or other movements, and add applicable security/title disclosures. This short note does not substitute for the complete asset and impairment policies.

    按固定資產登記冊填入類別、年期、剩餘價值及方法；工作簿的20%僅為例子。核對期初加添置減出售等於期末成本，並核對累計折舊變動及賬面值。期末賬面值須與財務狀況表、折舊與費用核對。如有減值、撥回、其他變動、抵押或業權限制，須擴充表格及披露。本頁不代替完整資產及減值政策。
