# Note 2(d) — Revenue recognition policies / 收益確認政策

**Allen lock 2026-09-19.** Simple SME-FRS modules · EN + 繁體中文.

**Rules**
- Select only live streams; must match **Note 3** categories.
- Common pack modules: **goods · services · interest · dividend**.
- Interest: recognised **as it accrues** (simple SME).
- Drop unused policy bullets when the related Note 3 row is dropped.
- Optional later (not locked here): rental, royalties, commission/agency.

---

## Sale of goods / 貨品銷售

### English

```
Revenue from sale of goods

Revenue from the sale of goods is recognised when the goods are delivered
and title has passed, provided that it is probable that the economic
benefits will flow to the Company and the revenue and costs can be
measured reliably.
```

### 中文

```
貨品銷售收入

貨品銷售收入於貨品交付且所有權轉移時確認，惟經濟利益很可能流入
本公司，且收入及成本能可靠計量。
```

## Dividend income / 股息收入

### English

```
Dividend income

Dividend income is recognised when the shareholder's right to receive
payment is established.
```
