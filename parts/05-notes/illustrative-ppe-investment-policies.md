# Note 2 — PPE & investments (cost model) / 物業設備及投資（成本模式）

**Allen lock 2026-09-19.** Cost model · EN + 繁體中文.

**Rules**
- Select only live modules; match **SoFP face** captions.
- **PPE**, **subsidiaries / associates / joint ventures**, and **equity investments** (other): all **at cost** — not fair value — in the common SME-FRS pack.
- Sub / asso / JV: **no equity accounting**.
- Equity investments: SME-FRS §6 cost model (LT cost − impairment; current lower of cost & NRV; listed market-value disclosure when not at MV).
- Depreciation **rate table** is case-specific — not locked here.
- Non-consolidation basis stays in the subsidiaries **note** (Note 2(c) cross-reference only) — see wiki / non-consolidation-placement.

## Property, plant and equipment / 物業、廠房及設備

### English

```
Property, plant and equipment

Property, plant and equipment are stated at cost less accumulated
depreciation and any accumulated impairment losses.

Depreciation is calculated on the straight-line method to write off
the cost of each asset over its estimated useful life.
```

### 中文

```
物業、廠房及設備

物業、廠房及設備按成本減累計折舊及任何累計減值虧損列賬。

折舊以直線法按各資產之估計可使用年期撇銷其成本。
```

## Investments in subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures / 於附屬、聯營及合營公司之投資

### English

```
Investments in subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures

Investments in subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures are stated
at cost less any accumulated impairment losses. The carrying amounts
are reviewed at each balance sheet date for indication of impairment.
Where an indication exists, an impairment loss is recognised in the
income statement to the extent the carrying amount exceeds recoverable
amount.
```

### 中文

```
於附屬公司、聯營公司及合營公司之投資

於附屬公司、聯營公司及合營公司之投資按成本扣除任何累計減值虧損
列賬。賬面值於各結算日檢討是否存在減值跡象。倘存在減值跡象，
且賬面值超出可收回金額，減值虧損於損益表確認。
```

## Equity investments / 股權投資

### English

```
Equity investments

Equity investments (other than subsidiaries, associates and joint
ventures) are initially measured at cost. Long-term equity investments
are subsequently carried at cost less accumulated impairment losses.
Current equity investments are subsequently carried at the lower of
cost and net realisable value.

For listed equity securities not carried at market value, market value
at the balance sheet date is disclosed. Unlisted equity securities are
carried at cost less impairment where quoted market prices are not
available and fair value cannot be reliably measured.
```

### 中文

```
股權投資

股權投資（附屬、聯營及合營公司除外）初步按成本計量。長期股權
投資其後按成本扣除累計減值虧損列賬。流動股權投資其後按成本與
可變現淨值兩者之較低者列賬。

倘上市股本證券並非按市值列賬，則披露其於結算日之市值。非上市
股本證券因並無公開市場報價且公允價值無法可靠計量，按成本扣除
減值列賬。
```
