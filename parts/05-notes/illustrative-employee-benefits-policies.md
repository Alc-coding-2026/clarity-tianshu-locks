# Note 2 — Employee benefits (short-term + MPF) / 僱員福利（短期及強積金）

**Allen lock 2026-10-10.** EN + 繁體中文. Restored from legacy house wording (P-20260718-01, approved 2026-07-18). Chinese is editorial house ZH, not an official HKICPA translation.

**Allen lock 2026-10-25 (HKT)** via chat **t410u** — **both** forms below are accepted and locked. Neither is must-only. Team may pick **Form Long** or **Form Short**. Do not invent a third.

**Letter / assembly (Allen lock 2026-10-12):** **Bold** heading. Letter is **sequential among live policies** on that pack. When the normal active set is present, this module usually lands as **Note 2(d) Employee benefits**. **One** lettered heading covers both Retirement benefits (MPF) and Short-term employee benefits — do **not** split into separate letters for short-term vs retirement. Sub-headings under the lettered heading have no separate letters. Form Short keeps the same single heading with no sub-heads.

**Rules**
- Include only when staff costs, directors' salaries or MPF contributions arise in the **current or comparative** year (or accrued staff liabilities).
- Drop this whole module when these are **nil in both years** (dormant / no employees / no staff costs / no MPF).
- **Defined contribution only** (Hong Kong MPF). No defined-benefit, ORSO, LSP, share-based payments, or long-service-payment policy in this common SME pack unless separately locked.
- **Both Form Long and Form Short are accepted.** Neither is must-only. Do not invent a third.
- ⚠️ A director’s **fee** attracts **no** MPF (office-holder, not employment). Do not extend this policy to directors’ fees. MPF applies to a director only under a separate salaried employment contract (then disclosed as salary / emoluments).
- Face amounts for staff / directors’ pay stay in their own notes — this page is Note 2 policy wording only.

**Basis:** SME-FRS Section 9 (Employee Benefits); Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Ordinance (Cap. 485).

## Form Long / 長式（既有鎖定）

**When:** default house specimen (legacy P-20260718-01). Cap. 485 MPF paragraph with vesting / independent fund wording, plus STEB sub-head.

### English

```
Employee benefits

Retirement benefits
The Company operates a defined contribution Mandatory Provident Fund retirement benefits
scheme (the "MPF Scheme") under the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Ordinance for all of
its employees. Contributions are made based on a percentage of the employees' relevant
income and are charged to profit or loss as they become payable in accordance with the
rules of the MPF Scheme. The assets of the MPF Scheme are held separately from those of
the Company in an independently administered fund. The Company's employer contributions
vest fully with the employees when contributed into the MPF Scheme.

Short-term employee benefits
Salaries, allowances and the cost of non-monetary benefits are accrued in the period in
which the associated services are rendered by employees.
```

### 中文（編輯譯文）

```
僱員福利

退休福利
本公司根據《強制性公積金計劃條例》為其所有僱員設立一項定額供款強制性公積金退休福利計劃
（「強積金計劃」）。供款按僱員有關入息的百分比計算，並根據強積金計劃的規則於應付時在損益
中扣除。強積金計劃的資產與本公司的資產分開持有，並由獨立管理的基金保管。本公司的僱主供款
於供入強積金計劃時全數歸屬僱員。

短期僱員福利
薪金、津貼及非貨幣福利成本於僱員提供有關服務的期間內累計入賬。
```

## Form Short / 短式（Bespoke alternate · Allen t410u）

**When:** short Bespoke-style alternate. One heading; two paragraphs (STEB then MPF). No Cap. 485 / vesting / independent-fund detail. Chinese is editorial house ZH from Allen paste 2026-10-25, not an official HKICPA translation.

### English

```
Employee benefits

Salaries, allowances and the cost of non-monetary benefits are accrued in the period in which the associated services are rendered by employees.

Contributions to the Mandatory Provident Fund Scheme are charged to profit or loss as they become payable.
```

### 中文（編輯譯文）

```
僱員福利

薪金、津貼及非貨幣福利之成本於僱員提供相關服務之期間內計提。

強制性公積金計劃之供款於應付時在損益中扣除。
```
