**Shared measurement line (SME-FRS cost model) / 共用計量句（SME-FRS 成本模式）** — append after the chosen basis block when SME-FRS applies:

```text
Investments in subsidiaries are stated at cost less accumulated impairment losses.

```

## Which track? / 選哪條路徑？

| Variant | When | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Cap.622 s.379(3)(a)** | Reporting company is a **wholly owned subsidiary** of another body corporate in the financial year | Group ownership / s.358(3) wholly-owned evidence |
| **B — Cap.622 s.379(3)(b)** | Reporting company is a **partially owned subsidiary**; directors used the **notice** route | Written notice to members ≥6 months before YE; as at 3 months before YE, no member written request for consolidated statements |
| **C — Cap.622 s.379(3)(c)** | Reporting company is a **partially owned subsidiary**; **all members** written agreement before YE end (2018 amendment) | Signed all-members agreement for **that year only**, dated before YE |
| **S — SME-FRS 19.1(c)** (house) | Company within **reporting exemption** and excluding subsidiaries under SME-FRS §19 (Cap.622 **s.381(2)** points to the standard) | Signed shareholder non-consol approval / consent on file |

Variants A–C answer Cap.622 when the company **is itself a subsidiary** of another body. Variant S is the existing house SME parent pack specimen (already on wiki Note 7).

## Variant A — Cap.622 s.379(3)(a) wholly owned subsidiary

**Use only when** the Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of another body corporate in the financial year.

### English

```text
These financial statements are the [separate / company-level] financial statements of the
Company. The Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of [PARENT NAME], a body corporate.
Pursuant to section 379(3)(a) of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), the directors are
not required to prepare consolidated financial statements for the financial year.
Accordingly, the Company has not prepared consolidated financial statements.
```

### 繁體中文（編輯譯文）

```text
本財務報表為本公司之[個別／公司層面]財務報表。本公司為法人團體[母公司名稱]之
全資附屬公司。根據《公司條例》（第622章）第379(3)(a)條，董事無須就本財政年度
編製綜合財務報表。因此，本公司並無編製綜合財務報表。
```

## Variant B — Cap.622 s.379(3)(b) partially owned subsidiary + notice

**Use only when** the Company is a partially owned subsidiary **and** the s.379(3)(b) notice procedure was completed for this financial year only.

### English

```text
These financial statements are the [separate / company-level] financial statements of the
Company. The Company is a partially owned subsidiary of [PARENT NAME], a body corporate.
At least six months before the end of the financial year, the directors notified the members
in writing of the directors’ intention not to prepare consolidated financial statements for
the financial year (and the notification did not relate to any other financial year). As at
a date falling three months before the end of the financial year, no member had responded
to the notification by giving the directors a written request for the preparation of
consolidated financial statements for the financial year. Pursuant to section 379(3)(b) of
the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), the directors are not required to prepare consolidated
financial statements for the financial year. Accordingly, the Company has not prepared
consolidated financial statements.

Copies of the notification [and related records] are retained on file.
```

### 繁體中文（編輯譯文）

```text
本財務報表為本公司之[個別／公司層面]財務報表。本公司為法人團體[母公司名稱]之
非全資附屬公司。董事已於財政年度結束前至少六個月，以書面通知各成員其擬不就
該財政年度編製綜合財務報表之意向（且該通知並不關乎任何其他財政年度）。於財政
年度結束前三個月當日，並無成員就該通知向董事發出書面要求，要求就該財政年度
編製綜合財務報表。根據《公司條例》（第622章）第379(3)(b)條，董事無須就本財政
年度編製綜合財務報表。因此，本公司並無編製綜合財務報表。

有關通知[及相關紀錄]已留存於檔案。
```

## Variant C — Cap.622 s.379(3)(c) all members written agreement

**Use only when** the Company is a partially owned subsidiary **and** all members agreed in writing **before the end of the financial year** that consolidated statements will not be prepared for **that year only**.

### English

```text
These financial statements are the [separate / company-level] financial statements of the
Company. The Company is a partially owned subsidiary of [PARENT NAME], a body corporate.
All members of the Company agreed in writing before the end of the financial year that
consolidated financial statements would not be prepared for the financial year, and the
agreement does not relate to any other financial year. Pursuant to section 379(3)(c) of
the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), the directors are not required to prepare consolidated
financial statements for the financial year. Accordingly, the Company has not prepared
consolidated financial statements.

The written agreement of [the sole member / all members] has been obtained and is
retained on file.
```

### 繁體中文（編輯譯文）

```text
本財務報表為本公司之[個別／公司層面]財務報表。本公司為法人團體[母公司名稱]之
非全資附屬公司。本公司全體成員已於財政年度結束前以書面同意，不會就該財政年度
編製綜合財務報表，且該同意並不關乎任何其他財政年度。根據《公司條例》（第622章）
第379(3)(c)條，董事無須就本財政年度編製綜合財務報表。因此，本公司並無編製綜合
財務報表。

本公司已取得[唯一成員／全體成員]之書面同意並留存於檔案。
```

## Variant S — SME-FRS 19.1(c) (existing house specimen)

**Already on wiki** Note 7. Restated for one-pack comparison. Cap.622 path is **s.381(2)** + SME-FRS §19 — **not** s.379(3).

### English

```text
These financial statements are the separate financial statements of the
Company. The Company has not prepared consolidated financial statements,
as all shareholders of the Company have agreed that consolidated
financial statements need not be prepared. This is in accordance with
Section 19.1(c) of the Small and Medium-sized Entity Financial Reporting
Standard ("SME-FRS").

A written approval from [the sole shareholder / all shareholders] has
been obtained and is retained on file.

Investments in subsidiaries are stated at cost less accumulated
impairment losses.
```

### 繁體中文

```text
本財務報表為本公司之個別財務報表。由於本公司全體股東已同意
毋須編製合併財務報表，故本公司並無編製合併財務報表。此乃
按照《中小型企業財務報告準則》第19.1(c)條作出。

本公司已取得[唯一股東／全體股東]之書面批准並留存於檔案。

於附屬公司之投資按成本扣除累計減值虧損列賬。
```
