# 關聯方 / Related parties

**Allen 已批准 / Approved by Allen · 2026-09-13（英文及繁體中文 / EN + 繁體中文）** 中文為已批准的編輯譯文，並非 HKICPA 官方譯文。

**適用 / Scope:** Company-level SME-FRS; sales/purchases with a company controlled by the same individual, plus related balances. This specimen requires factual tailoring; common directorship alone is insufficient. 公司層面 SME-FRS；與受同一人士控制的公司進行銷購及相關結餘。單憑共同董事身份並不足夠。

??? note "使用及來源 / Application and sources"

    Include all applicable parties/transaction types, not only sales and purchases. Keep distinct balance terms separate. Reconcile gross balances and provisions to the ledger; do not net receivables/payables in this note. Do not assert arm’s-length terms without evidence. Directors may not be the only key management personnel. Add the parent and ultimate controlling company names where relevant, even if there were no transactions; remove the common-individual paragraph if it misdescribes the actual relationship.

    須包括所有適用關聯方及交易類型，並分別說明不同結餘條款。核對總額及撥備，不在本附註抵銷應收應付款。未有證據不得聲稱按公平交易條款進行。主要管理人員不限於董事。適用時加入母公司及最終控股公司名稱，即使並無交易；關係描述須符合事實。

    Source: workbook `54 - RPT!A1:E5` and `1 - REPORT INFO!B108:C108` supply the sales/purchases layout and common-control input. Balances, terms, guarantees, doubtful debts and key management remuneration are editorial additions to address SME-FRS §16.5 and §16.4, not fields recovered from that two-row sheet. [HKICPA SME-FRF & SME-FRS, March 2025](https://www.hkicpa.org.hk/-/media/HKICPA-Website/Members-Handbook/volumeII/smefrf1221.pdf), §§16.2–16.5 (indexed printed p.66); see [verification record](../review/illustrative-notes-provenance.md).
