## Cap.622 vs SME-FRS — do not mix labels / 勿混用標籤

Allen’s shorthand (wholly own / 75%+consent / 100% consent) maps to **different** Ordinance provisions. Cite the correct track; do not invent a 75% Note 7 non-consol specimen.

| Label people say | What Cap.622 / SME-FRS actually is | Use for Note 7 non-consol? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Wholly own** | **Cap.622 s.379(3)(a)** — the **reporting company** is a **wholly owned subsidiary** of another body corporate in the financial year (see s.358(3) definition of wholly owned subsidiary) | Only when that fact is true. Not “parent owns 100% of its subs”. |
| **Partially owned sub + notice** | **Cap.622 s.379(3)(b)** — reporting company is a **partially owned subsidiary**; directors notify members in writing ≥6 months before YE; as at 3 months before YE no member has written requesting consolidated statements | Cap.622 exemption track when company is itself a sub. |
| **3. 100% consent (members)** | **Cap.622 s.379(3)(c)** (2018 amendment) — partially owned subsidiary: **all members** agree in writing **before YE end** that consolidated statements will not be prepared for that year only | Cap.622 exemption track when company is itself a sub. |
| **2. 75%+consent** | **Cap.622 s.360** — **reporting exemption** for eligible private company / group (resolution by ≥75% voting rights; remaining do not vote against). Related: **s.359(1)(b)** standalone private co with no sub / not a sub needs **all members** written agreement for reporting exemption that year | **No** — this is reporting-exemption eligibility, **not** Note 7 non-consol basis. |
| **House SME pack (parent excluding subs)** | **Cap.622 s.381(2)** (reporting exemption year) + **SME-FRS §19** (esp. house specimen **19.1(c)** all shareholders / sole shareholder + written approval on file) | Yes — existing wiki Note 7 specimen. Citation stays **SME-FRS 19.1(c)** (legacy: not Cap.622 s.359, not old S.9.3). |

**Sources:** Cap.622 Part 9 (Companies Registry Part 9 text / elegislation s.379); 2018 amendment introducing s.379(3)(c); existing wiki `n06-09-assets.md` Note 7 + `n02-basis-policies.md` evidence gate.

**No new full EN/ZH Cap.622 379 specimens are locked here.** Add only when Allen locks them. Until then: map + evidence; use existing 19.1(c) specimen only when that route’s evidence is on file.

    1. Identify track: Cap.622 379 (company is a sub) vs Cap.622 381(2)+SME-FRS 19 (reporting exemption parent) vs reporting-exemption eligibility only (359/360). / 先識別路徑。
