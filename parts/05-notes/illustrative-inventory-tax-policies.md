# Note 2 — Inventories & income tax policies / 存貨及所得稅政策

**Allen lock 2026-09-19.** EN + 繁體中文.

**Rules**
- **Inventories:** include only when live on SoFP; pick **FIFO** or **weighted average** for the case.
- **Income tax:** current tax only; **no deferred tax**. Two-tier / reduction / payable wording lives in **Note 5** (not repeated here).
- Overseas tax: out of this common SME pack unless separately locked.

---

## Inventories / 存貨

### English

```
Inventories

Inventories are stated at the lower of cost and net realisable value.
Cost is determined on a [FIFO / weighted average] basis and comprises
all costs of purchase, costs of conversion and other costs incurred in
bringing the inventories to their present location and condition. Net
realisable value is the estimated selling price in the ordinary course
of business less the estimated costs of completion and the estimated
costs necessary to make the sale.
```

### 中文

```
存貨

存貨按成本與可變現淨值兩者之較低者列賬。成本按[先進先出／加權平均]
法釐定，包括所有採購成本、加工成本及使存貨達致目前地點及狀況所產生
之其他成本。可變現淨值為日常業務過程中之估計售價減估計完成成本及
估計銷售所需成本。
```

## Income tax / 所得稅

### English

```
Income tax

Income tax expense comprises current tax. Current tax is the expected
tax payable on the taxable income for the year, using tax rates enacted
or substantively enacted at the balance sheet date, and any adjustment
to tax payable in respect of previous years.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are not recognised.
```

### 中文

```
所得稅

所得稅開支包括即期稅項。即期稅項乃按於結算日已頒布或實質上已頒布
之稅率，就年內應課稅收入計算之預期應付稅項，以及對過往年度應付
稅項之任何調整。

不確認遞延稅項資產及負債。
```
