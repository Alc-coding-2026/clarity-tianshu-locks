# Note 2(b) — Going concern Path A / 持續經營（路徑 A）

**Allen lock 2026-09-19** (parent / holding-company support variant topped up from HRise inactive golden face).  
Shareholder + director variants already in wiki `n02-basis-policies`; parent variant locked here for inactive/subsidiary shells.

**Rules (all Path A)**
- Net liabilities at BS date; support letter(s) resolve GC → **Clean UQ**; **no** MURGC EOM; **no** “notwithstanding the material uncertainty”.
- “as at the balance sheet date” (not an explicit calendar date in the net-liabilities sentence).
- Single net-liabilities figure.
- Path 0 (net assets): **delete** entire GC sub-section.
- Path B (material external creditors unresolved): separate MURGC wording — not this page.

---

## Variant P — Immediate / ultimate holding company support *(HRise top-up)*

**When:** subsidiary / group shell; GC support from **holding company** (not shareholders/directors).

### English

```
Going concern

The financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.

As at the balance sheet date, the Company's total liabilities exceeded
its total assets by HK$[NET_LIABILITIES_AMOUNT].

The Company's immediate and ultimate holding company, [HOLDING COMPANY
NAME], has provided a letter of support confirming its commitment to
provide adequate financial support to enable the Company to meet its
obligations as they fall due for a period of not less than twelve months
from the date of approval of these financial statements.

Based on the letter of financial support, the directors are satisfied
that the Company will have sufficient working capital to meet its
financial obligations as and when they fall due. Accordingly, the
financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.
```

### 中文

```
持續經營

本財務報表已按持續經營基準編製。

於結算日，本公司之總負債超出其總資產港幣[淨負債金額]。

本公司之直接及最終控股公司[控股公司名稱]已提供支援函，確認其承諾
提供充足之財務支持，以使本公司能夠在不超過本財務報表批准日期起計
十二個月之期間內償還到期之債務。

基於該財務支援函，董事信納本公司將有足夠營運資金以於其到期時償還
其財務責任。因此，本財務報表已按持續經營基準編製。
```

**Provenance:** HRise Limited FINAL CLIENT (inactive single-year). Singular **letter** (holding company). If immediate ≠ ultimate, name the entity that actually gave the letter; do not invent dual support.

## Variant S — Shareholders' letters of support *(wiki Path A)*

### English

```
Going concern

The financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.

As at the balance sheet date, the Company's total liabilities exceeded
its total assets by HK$[NET_LIABILITIES_AMOUNT].

The Company's shareholders have provided letters of support confirming
their commitment to provide adequate financial support to enable the
Company to meet its obligations as they fall due for a period of not
less than twelve months from the date of approval of these financial
statements.

Based on the letters of financial support, the directors are satisfied
that the Company will have sufficient working capital to meet its
financial obligations as and when they fall due. Accordingly, the
financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.
```

### 中文

```
持續經營

本財務報表已按持續經營基準編製。

於結算日，本公司之總負債超出其總資產港幣[淨負債金額]。

本公司股東已提供支援函，確認彼等承諾提供充足之財務支持，以使本
公司能夠在不超過本財務報表批准日期起計十二個月之期間內償還到期
之債務。

基於該等財務支援函，董事信納本公司將有足夠營運資金以於其到期時
償還其財務責任。因此，本財務報表已按持續經營基準編製。
```
