# Note — Fellow subsidiary costs not recharged / 同系附屬公司成本不收回

**Allen lock 2026-09-19.** Top-up from HRise inactive golden face · EN + editorial 繁體中文.

**Rules**
- Use when fellow subsidiaries **bore costs without recharge** and those costs are **not** recognised as expenses or liabilities of the Company.
- Place in related-party / expenses note as facts require — do not invent recharge arrangements.
- Does **not** replace full related-party disclosure or the long Note 2 RP definition.

---

## English

```
Other costs were borne by fellow subsidiaries without recharge to the
Company and are therefore not recognised as expenses or liabilities of
the Company.
```

## 中文

```
其他成本由同系附屬公司承擔而並無向本公司收取，故並不確認為本公司
之開支或負債。
```
