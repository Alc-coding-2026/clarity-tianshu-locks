> **Chinese:** editorial 繁體中文, not an official HKICPA translation.

**Cause:** dividends were distributed in excess of available reserves, against a negative reserve / accumulated losses. Amounts due to shareholders and withholding tax payables that had been recognised were incorrectly recorded and are reversed to nil.

**IRD (meta — not face wording):** reversing the withholding-tax payable needs evidence that the prior-year accrual was wrong. Do not copy this sentence into the note.

---

## When to use

Prior-period error. Restate comparatives under SME-FRS Section 7, **including the opening balances of the earliest period presented** (opening / last-last), not only the prior year-end.

## Do not use

- Valid liabilities waived, forgiven or cash-settled in the current year. That is not this face. Optional unlocked Path B only: [`note-dues-wht-extinguishment-draft.md`](./note-dues-wht-extinguishment-draft.md).
- Case 1 or Case 2.
- The 恆美麗 “no effect on the loss” closing line as a substitute for the closing below.

## `(Restated)` marks

Spelling is `(Restated)`, never `(Restate)`. Space before the bracket.

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Earliest period presented — opening | Restate the opening balances. Label `(Restated)` where those figures changed. |
| Statement of financial position — prior-year column | Add `(Restated)`. |
| SOCIE | `Balance as at [OPENING] (Restated)` and `Balance as at [PY DATE] (Restated)` when those equity amounts changed. |
| Notes whose prior-year figures changed | Add `(Restated)`, including the shareholder line on Notes 13/15 and the withholding-tax line. |
| Notes unchanged | No label. |
