### AR-P — Accounts receivables / 應收賬款（sample-book based）

#### English

```
Accounts receivables

Accounts receivables and other receivables are stated at estimated
realisable value after each debt has been considered individually.
Where the payment of a debt becomes doubtful a provision is made
and charged to the income statement.
```

#### 中文（編輯譯文）

```
應收賬款

應收賬款及其他應收款項於逐項考慮各項債項後，按估計可變現價值列賬。
倘債項之付款成為呆賬，則作出撥備並於損益表扣除。
```

**Align with house face:** SoFP / Note 13 caption remains **Accounts and other receivables** / **應收賬款及其他應收款項** (locked). Policy may say “Accounts receivables” as the short policy heading, or expand the first sentence to “Accounts and other receivables…” for exact face alignment.  
政策標題可用「應收賬款」短稱，或改為「應收賬款及其他應收款項」以與面列完全一致。

### AP-P — Accounts payables / 應付賬款（editorial house — not in Appendix 2）

#### English

```
Accounts payables

Accounts payables for goods and services received are recognised
when the Company has a present obligation arising from past events
and are stated at the amounts payable in the ordinary course of
business.
```

#### 中文（編輯譯文）

```
應付賬款

就已收取貨品及服務產生之應付賬款，於本公司因過往事項而承擔現有
責任時確認，並按日常業務中應付之金額列賬。
```

**House defaults (locked 2026-10-02):** keep AP-P as editorial house wording; AR cites sample-book 1(k) with “Trade→Accounts” swap only; short policy heading “Accounts receivables” is acceptable (face remains Accounts and other receivables).
