??? note "使用及來源 / Application and sources"

    Tie share numbers to the register of members and capital amounts to the accounting records and statement of financial position. Do not equate the number of shares with the capital amount. This specimen makes no authorised-capital or par-value assertion. If there were issues, redemptions, reductions, unpaid amounts or multiple classes, prepare a movement schedule and fact-specific disclosures instead; this no-movement specimen is inapplicable.

    股份數目須與成員登記冊核對；股本金額須與會計記錄及財務狀況表核對。不得假定股數等於股本金額。本範例不就法定股本或面值作出聲明。如有發行、贖回、減資、未繳股款或多類股份，須另編變動表及具體披露。
