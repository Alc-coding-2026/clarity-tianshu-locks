# 股本 / Share capital

**Allen 已批准 / Approved by Allen · 2026-09-13（英文及繁體中文 / EN + 繁體中文）** 中文為已批准的編輯譯文，並非 HKICPA 官方譯文。

**Allen lock 2026-10-25 (HKT)** via chat widget **t413s1618** — **three** face forms below are accepted and locked. Neither is must-only. Team may pick **Form 4-column**, **Form A** (Bespoke two columns), or **Form B** (paren comparative). Do not invent a fourth.

**適用 / Scope:** Hong Kong company limited by shares; one fully paid ordinary share class. Company-level SME-FRS specimen. 香港股份有限公司，只有一類繳足普通股；公司層面 SME-FRS 範例。

**Rules**
- **All three forms accepted.** Neither is must-only. Do not invent a fourth.
- **Form 4-column** (existing Allen 2026-09-13 lock) — four-column no-movement table (shares + amounts for CY and PY; “At beginning and end of period” / 「期初及期末」). Fits when there is **no movement** in either period.
- **Form A — Bespoke two columns** (Allen **t412u**) — year headers `[CY] · [PY]`, one share-description line, then amount pair. Allen’s source face used a no-movement 1-share example; use when presenting CY/PY under two year columns.
- **Form B — paren comparative** (Allen **t413u**) — one line: `[CY shares] ordinary shares ([PY year]: [PY shares] ordinary shares) [CY amt] [PY amt]`. Allen’s source face showed **different** CY vs PY share counts; may be used when the share-count comparative is shown in parentheses. Do **not** treat this page as a full movement schedule.
- Multi-class shares, issues, redemptions, reductions, unpaid amounts → prepare a **movement schedule** and fact-specific disclosures; these face forms alone are inapplicable (unchanged from 2026-09-13 note).
- Tie share numbers to the register of members and capital amounts to the accounting records and statement of financial position. Do not equate the number of shares with the capital amount. No authorised-capital or par-value assertion in these specimens.

## Form A — Bespoke two columns / 兩欄（Allen t412u）

**When:** Bespoke-style alternate. Year headers as `[CY] · [PY]`; one ordinary-share description line; amount pair. Chinese is editorial house ZH, not an official HKICPA translation.

### English

```
[N] SHARE CAPITAL

Issued and fully paid ordinary shares:
[CY] · [PY]
[N] ordinary share[s]
[CY_AMT] · [PY_AMT]
```

**Example (Allen t412u face):**
```
Issued and fully paid ordinary shares:
2025 · 2024
1 ordinary share
1.00 · 1.00
```

### 中文（編輯譯文）

```
[N] 股本

已發行及繳足普通股：
[本年度] · [上年度]
[N]股普通股
[本年度金額] · [上年度金額]

**示例：**
```
已發行及繳足普通股：

## Form B — paren comparative / 括號比較（Allen t413u）

**When:** paren-comparative alternate. One line with CY share count, PY year + share count in parentheses, then CY and PY amounts. Chinese is editorial house ZH, not an official HKICPA translation.

### English

```
[N] SHARE CAPITAL

Issued and fully paid ordinary shares:
[CY_SHARES] ordinary shares ([PY_YEAR]: [PY_SHARES] ordinary shares)  [CY_AMT]  [PY_AMT]
```

**Example (Allen t413u face):**
```
100 ordinary shares (2024: 50 ordinary shares)  100  50
```

### 中文（編輯譯文）

```
[N] 股本

已發行及繳足普通股：
[本年度股數]股普通股（[上年度年份]：[上年度股數]股普通股）  [本年度金額]  [上年度金額]
```

**示例：**
```
100股普通股（2024：50股普通股）  100  50
```
