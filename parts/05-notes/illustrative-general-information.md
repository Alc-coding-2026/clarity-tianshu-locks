# 一般資料 / General information

**Allen 已批准 / Approved by Allen · 2026-09-13（英文及繁體中文 / EN + 繁體中文）** 中文為已批准的編輯譯文，並非 HKICPA 官方譯文。 / Chinese is an approved editorial translation, not an official HKICPA translation.

**適用 / Scope:** Hong Kong private company limited by shares; company-level SME-FRS statements, with no subsidiaries. 本範例適用於香港私人股份有限公司，按 SME-FRS 編製公司層面財務報表，且並無附屬公司。

=== "English"

    ```text
    [1] GENERAL INFORMATION

    [Company name] (the “Company”) is incorporated and domiciled in Hong Kong and is a private company limited by shares. Its registered office is at [registered office address]. Its principal place of business is at [business address].

    The Company’s principal activity is [specific principal activity].

    These financial statements cover the period from [period start] to [period end] and are presented in [presentation currency].
    ```

=== "繁體中文（建議譯文）"

    ```text
    [1] 一般資料

    [公司名稱]（「本公司」）為於香港註冊成立並以香港為註冊所在地的私人股份有限公司。註冊辦事處位於[註冊辦事處地址]，主要營業地點位於[營業地址]。

    本公司的主要業務為[具體主要業務]。

    本財務報表涵蓋[期間開始日]至[期間結束日]，並以[呈列貨幣]呈列。
    ```

??? note "使用及來源 / Application and sources"

    Verify incorporation, domicile, legal form, addresses, actual activity and the reporting period. If the business address is the registered office, combine the two sentences. Do not convert the workbook’s example “not commenced operation” into a factual conclusion. Add parent/ultimate controlling company particulars where applicable; see the related-party specimen. Unequal reporting periods need a specific comparability explanation. Where presentation and reporting currencies differ, disclose the reporting currency and reason for that difference.

    核實註冊地、註冊所在地、法律形式、地址、實際業務及報告期間。如營業地址與註冊辦事處相同，可合併兩句。不得把工作簿的未開業例子當作事實。適用時加入母公司及最終控股公司資料；期間長短不同時須另作可比性說明。如呈列貨幣與報告貨幣不同，須披露報告貨幣及差異原因。
