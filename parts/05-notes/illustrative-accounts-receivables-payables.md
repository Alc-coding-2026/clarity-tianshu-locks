# Notes 13 & 15 — Accounts and Other Receivables / Payables

**Allen lock 2026-09-14.** Working bible specimen. Entry port for BS streamlining.

**Rules**
- SoFP face shows only **Accounts and other receivables** / **Accounts and other payables**.
- Fold into these notes (inline lines): shareholder, holding company, related company, subsidiary, associate, joint venture, and **directors** (one aggregate line each).
- Directors aggregate line uses plural **directors** always; name-by-name detail lives in **Note 11 (s.383(1)(d))** and must **tie**.
- Naming: **Accounts receivables** / **Accounts payables** (plural). **預收款項** (not 預收賬款). **Accruals** or **Accrued expenses** both accepted. **Rental deposits** separate from other **Deposits**. Include **Prepayments** and **Receipts in advance**.
- Related-party disclosure still required in Note 18 as applicable.
- Omit nil lines in a real pack; keep placeholders here for the template.
