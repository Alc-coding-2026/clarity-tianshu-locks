# Commitments, contingencies, subsequent events & authorisation / 承擔、或然事項、期後事項及批准

**Allen lock 2026-10-02.** EN + editorial 繁體中文. Chinese is **not** an official HKICPA translation.  
Includes OL three-band **P-20260619-01**, capital commitments, contingencies, subsequent events and authorisation. Note numbers remain **`[N]`** placeholders.

**Scope / 適用：** Eligible SME-FRS Hong Kong private company limited by shares; company-level; normal annual period; **going concern**.  
符合 SME-FRS 資格之香港私人股份有限公司；公司層面；正常年度；**持續經營**。

**Not this page / 不屬本頁：** Deregistration / cessation subsequent-events Variant B and Note 2 break-up basis stay on their locked pages — cross-ref only; do not duplicate.  
撤銷登記／結業之路徑 B 期後事項及附註2變現基礎已鎖定於他頁 — 只交叉引用，勿重複。

---

## Rules / 選用規則

- Choose **each module independently** after reviewing contracts, board minutes, litigation files and events through the **actual authorisation date**.  
  須核查合約、董事會紀錄、訴訟檔案及截至**實際批准日期**之期後事項，**逐模組**選用。
- **Nil commitments ≠ nil contingencies ≠ nil subsequent events.** Never cascade a “nil” conclusion across modules.  
  **無承擔 ≠ 無或然事項 ≠ 無期後事項。** 不得將某一模組之「無」推論至其他模組。
- Operating-lease amounts table is a **framework requirement when applicable**. House lock: wiki **P-20260619-01** **three-band** layout (within 1 year / 2nd–5th years inclusive / after 5 years). Capital-commitment and contingency specimens are **house disclosure practice** when facts require — do **not** invent a standalone SME-FRS capital-commitment mandate beyond these specimens.  
  經營租賃金額表於適用時屬**準則要求**。行內鎖定：wiki **P-20260619-01** **三段式**（一年內／第二至第五年包括首尾兩年／五年以後）。資本承擔及或然事項範例於事實需要時為**行內披露實務** — **勿**在本標本以外另行發明獨立之 SME-FRS 資本承擔強制政策。
- Adjusting events: **adjust** amounts and update related disclosures (SME-FRS §§17.1, 17.7) — a subsequent-events paragraph alone is not enough.  
  調整事項須**調整金額**並更新相關披露 — 僅披露一段期後事項並不足夠。
- Going-concern-changing events (§17.6) and dereg / break-up paths are **outside** these going-concern specimens.  
  影響持續經營基礎之事項及撤銷登記／變現路徑**不屬**本頁持續經營範例範圍。
- Note numbers remain **`[N]`** placeholders until a later house-sequence lock.  
  附註編號維持 **`[N]`** 佔位，待其後編定套裝次序。

## 2. Operating lease commitments (lessee) / 經營租賃承擔（承租人）

**Status: Allen lock 2026-10-02** — wiki `commitments.md` ⭐ **P-20260619-01** three maturity bands.  
**狀態：Allen 鎖定 2026-10-02** — wiki `commitments.md` ⭐ **P-20260619-01** 三段到期。

**House layout (P-20260619-01):** When the Company is a **lessee** under **non-cancellable** operating leases, disclose future minimum lease payments in the **three** bands below (within one year / in the second to fifth years inclusive / after five years). Delete a nil **After five years** row when most SME leases ≤ 5 years.  
**行內格式（P-20260619-01）：** 本公司作為**不可撤銷**經營租賃之**承租人**時，按下述**三段**披露未來最低租賃付款（一年內／第二至第五年包括首尾兩年／五年以後）。多數 SME 租約 ≤ 5 年時，刪除為零之「五年以後」列。

Assess lease classification first. Cancellable / month-to-month tenancies stay as rent expense only — they do **not** enter this note. This note does **not** replace any rentals expense line elsewhere (e.g. operating lease rentals in a PBT / expense note).  
先核實租賃分類。可隨時取消／按月續租之租約只作租金開支，**不**入本附註。本附註**不**取代其他附註之租金開支披露（例如 PBT／費用附註之經營租賃租金）。

```

#### 中文

```

於報告期末，本公司根據不可撤銷經營租賃須於日後支付之最低租賃款項總額如下：

| | [本年度] | [上年度] |
|:---|---:|---:|

Reconcile totals to lease contracts and maturity dates; include comparatives; do not double-count rent already accrued as a liability. Delete the **After five years** / **五年以後** row when nil. Disclose only the **non-cancellable** portion.

## 3. Contingent liabilities / 或然負債

**Framework (SME-FRS §§10.18–10.22):** Disclose contingent liabilities unless the possibility of an outflow is **remote**. Do not recognise a provision when the obligation is not probable or cannot be measured reliably — disclose instead.  
**準則（§§10.18–10.22）：** 除非經濟利益外流之可能性屬**極微**，否則須披露或然負債。義務並非很可能發生或無法可靠計量時，不確認撥備，改以披露處理。

### CL-A — Disclosure specimen / 披露範例

#### English

```
[N]. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES

At [YE DATE], the Company had contingent liabilities as follows:

[Describe nature — e.g. a legal claim / guarantee / performance bond]
in the amount of approximately HK$[XXX,XXX] ([PY]: HK$[XXX,XXX]).
In the opinion of the directors, it is not probable that an outflow of
economic benefits will be required, [and/or the amount cannot be
measured reliably]. Accordingly, no provision has been made in these
financial statements.
```

#### 中文

```
[N]. 或然負債

於[年結日]，本公司之或然負債如下：

[說明性質 — 例如法律申索／擔保／履約保證]，金額約為港幣[XXX,XXX]元
（上年度：港幣[XXX,XXX]元）。董事認為，經濟利益外流並非很可能發生
[及／或金額無法可靠計量]，故並無於本財務報表作出撥備。
```

Rewrite the nature sentence to the **actual** claim / guarantee / bond. Never leave bracketed examples in issued FS.  
須按**實際**申索／擔保／保證改寫性質句。已發出財務報表不得留下方括號示例。

---

### CL-N — Verified nil / 已核實為無

#### English

```
[N]. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES

The Company had no contingent liabilities at [YE DATE] ([PY]: nil).
```

#### 中文

```
[N]. 或然負債

於[年結日]，本公司並無或然負債（上年度：無）。
```

Optional module — use only after litigation, guarantee and bank-facility review. Remote items need not be listed.  
可選模組 — 僅於完成訴訟、擔保及銀行融資核查後使用。可能性極微者無須列示。

## 4. Contingent assets / 或然資產

**Framework:** Disclose contingent assets when an inflow is **probable**. Do not recognise until realisation is virtually certain. Most inactive / simple SME packs **omit** this note.  
**準則：** 經濟利益流入屬**很可能**時披露或然資產；在幾乎肯定可實現前不得確認。多數不活動／簡單 SME 套裝**省略**本附註。

### CA-A — Disclosure specimen / 披露範例（罕見）

#### English

```
[N]. CONTINGENT ASSETS

At [YE DATE], the Company had a contingent asset arising from
[describe nature — e.g. a claim / insurance recovery] of approximately
HK$[XXX,XXX]. An inflow of economic benefits is considered probable
but is not virtually certain; accordingly, no asset has been recognised.
```

#### 中文

```
[N]. 或然資產

於[年結日]，本公司因[說明性質 — 例如申索／保險索償]而產生或然資產，
金額約為港幣[XXX,XXX]元。經濟利益流入屬很可能但並非幾乎肯定，
故並無確認為資產。
```

### CA-N — Default

**Omit the note** unless facts require CA-A. A separate “nil contingent assets” sentence is **not** proposed as house default.  
除事實需要 CA-A 外，**省略本附註**。本頁不建議以「並無或然資產」作為預設句。

## 5. Events after the end of the reporting period / 報告期結束後事項

**Preferred heading** (align with locked dereg Variant B): **Events after the end of the reporting period**. “Subsequent events” remains acceptable in live packs that already use it.  
**建議標題**（與已鎖定撤銷登記路徑 B 一致）：**報告期結束後事項**。若現行套裝已用 “Subsequent events”，仍可接受。

### Guidance — adjusting vs non-adjusting / 指引 — 調整與非調整

| | Adjusting / 調整事項 | Non-adjusting / 非調整事項 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Condition | Evidence of conditions **that existed** at the reporting date (§§17.1–17.4) | Conditions **arose after** the reporting date (§17.3) |
| Accounting | **Adjust** amounts recognised; update related disclosures (§17.7) | **Do not adjust** recognised amounts; disclose if material (§§17.8–17.9) |
| Examples (orientation only) | Settlement of a court case confirming a present obligation at YE; discovery of fraud/error at YE | Dividend **proposed/declared after** YE; major business combination after YE; fire/flood after YE |
| This pack | Usually **no separate SE note** — effect is in the numbers | Use **SE-D** / **SE-M** below |

Going-concern-changing events → §17.6 / GC Path or dereg pack — **not** SE-D/SE-M/SE-N.  
影響持續經營基礎 → §17.6／持續經營路徑或撤銷登記套裝 — **不用** SE-D／SE-M／SE-N。

---

### SE-D — Dividend proposed after year end (non-adjusting) / 年結後建議末期股息（非調整）

**Use when** directors propose a final dividend **after** the reporting date and before authorisation. Do **not** recognise as a liability at YE.  
**適用於**董事於報告日期**後**、批准前建議末期股息。年結日**不得**確認為負債。

#### English

```
[N]. EVENTS AFTER THE END OF THE REPORTING PERIOD

On [DATE], the directors proposed a final dividend of HK$[X] per
ordinary share, amounting to HK$[XXX,XXX] in aggregate, in respect of
the year ended [YE DATE]. The proposed dividend is a non-adjusting
event and has not been recognised as a liability in these financial
statements.
```

#### 中文

```
[N]. 報告期結束後事項

於[日期]，董事就截至[年結日]止年度建議派發末期股息每股普通股
港幣[X]元，合共港幣[XXX,XXX]元。該建議股息屬非調整事項，並無於
本財務報表確認為負債。
```

If no dividend is proposed, do **not** insert a “no dividend proposed” line into this module by default — silence is enough (standalone dividend note is out of pack).  
若無建議股息，預設**不必**加入「並無建議股息」一句 — 沉默即可（獨立股息附註已不在套裝內）。

### SE-M — Other material non-adjusting event / 其他重大非調整事項

#### English

```
[N]. EVENTS AFTER THE END OF THE REPORTING PERIOD

On [DATE], [describe the event and its nature]. The event arose after
[YE DATE] and does not require adjustment of the amounts recognised at
that date. Its estimated financial effect is [currency and amount, with
a description of the affected assets, liabilities, income or expenses].
```

#### 中文

```
[N]. 報告期結束後事項

於[日期]，[說明事項及其性質]。該事項於[年結日]後發生，毋須調整於
該日確認之金額。其估計財務影響為[貨幣及金額，並說明受影響之資產、
負債、收入或費用]。
```

If no estimate can be made, replace **only** the last sentence:

**EN:** `An estimate of the financial effect cannot be made because [reason].`  
**中文:** `由於[原因]，無法估計該事項之財務影響。`

Disclose **each** significant category separately (§17.8).  
須按§17.8就**每一**重大類別分別披露。

### SE-N — Verified nil (material non-adjusting) / 已核實並無重大非調整事項

#### English

```
[N]. EVENTS AFTER THE END OF THE REPORTING PERIOD

There are no material non-adjusting events subsequent to the end of
the reporting period that require disclosure in these financial
statements.
```

#### 中文

```
[N]. 報告期結束後事項

於報告期結束後，並無其他須於本財務報表披露之重大非調整事項。
```

Use only after reviewing the period through the **actual** authorisation date. Do **not** combine with SE-D or SE-M. SE-N does **not** assert that no adjusting event occurred.  
僅於核查至**實際**批准日期後使用。不得與 SE-D 或 SE-M 並用。SE-N **不**表示並無調整事項發生。

## 6. Authorisation of financial statements / 財務報表之批准

**Framework (§17.10) — always disclose**, even when SE-N applies: the date when the financial statements were authorised for issue, and who gave that authorisation.  
**準則（§17.10）— 必須披露**（即使採用 SE-N）：財務報表獲批准發出之日期，以及作出批准之人士／機構。

### AU — Board authorisation (common SME) / 董事會批准（常見）

#### English

```
[N]. AUTHORISATION OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

These financial statements were authorised for issue by the Board of
Directors on [DATE].
```

#### 中文

```
[N]. 財務報表之批准

本財務報表已於[日期]獲董事會批准發出。
```

Use the board specimen **only if** the board authorised issue. If owners or others have power to amend after issue, **add**:

**EN:** `[Identify owners or other persons] have the power to amend these financial statements after issuance.`  
**中文:** `[列明擁有人或其他人士]有權於本財務報表發出後修改本財務報表。`

Verify powers; do not assume either outcome.  
須核實權力，不得預設。
