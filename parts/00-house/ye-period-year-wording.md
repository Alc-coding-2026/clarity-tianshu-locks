# Year-end wording: period / year · 年結措辭：期間／年度

**Allen lock 2026-10-23.** Confirmed again **2026-11-04** (HKT) via Audit/天書. Allen GO 2026-11-04: put this on the tip.

This is a review rule for a **year-end / annual** case. It does not rewrite a locked face. First-period, multi-period and cessation packs still use their own from–to period wording.

## English

On a year-end / annual case, both are accepted:

- “year” and “period”
- “year end” and “period end”

The placeholder `[period / year]` may resolve either way.

Using “period” or “period end” on an annual pack is **not a defect**. Prefer one choice inside a single pack when that is easy. A mix of year and period in the same pack is **not a rematch flag**.

## 繁體中文

年結／年度個案，兩者均可接受：

- 「年度」與「期間」
- “year end” 與 “period end”（英文年結／期間結束措辭）

佔位符「[期間／年度]」可解析為「年度」或「期間」。

年度報告套裝使用「期間」**不是缺陷**。同一套裝內容易做到時，盡量只用一種。同一套裝混用「期間」與「年度」**不是 rematch flag**（不是覆核重配對標記）。

## What this does not change / 不改動的範圍

- Opening A (first or multi-period) still prefers “period” / 由開始日至結束日. Opening B (normal year) still reads “year ended” / 截至年結日止年度. Those remain the pick-and-paste faces.
- A cessation or other stub period still names the actual from–to period. Do not force “year” onto a period that is not a year.
- Do not treat this lock as a reason to rewrite an otherwise locked sentence.
