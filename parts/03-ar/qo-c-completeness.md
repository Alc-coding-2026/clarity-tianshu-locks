# AR-QO-C — Completeness (Bank-Statement-Based Records)

Live: https://siumon.eternalorigins.net/tianshu/03-ar/qo-c-completeness/

**Use when:** Company maintains records based on bank statements, not formal accounting system.

**S.407:** REQUIRED. RL must include “Qualified Opinion Acknowledgement.”  
Combined with QO-B (2-item): number as “1. … 2. …” — AR header “except for the possible effects of the matters described in paragraphs 1 and 2”.

Matrix row: **C — Bank-stmt records — 1 (completeness) — S.407 ✅**

## Basis for Qualified Opinion — EN

```
The Company maintains its records based on bank statements and supporting documents. Accordingly, completeness of revenue and cost of services can only be verified to the extent of bank deposits and payments respectively. Consequently, we were unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence over the completeness of revenue and cost of services and the elements making up the income statement.
```

## 保留意見基礎 — 中文

```
貴公司以銀行對賬單及相關文件為基礎維護記錄。因此，收入及服務成本之完整性僅可根據銀行存款及付款進行核實。故本核數師未能就收入及服務成本以及構成損益表各項目之完整性獲取充分適當之審計憑證。
```

## Notes for ACTIVE trading (e.g. 信昌行 / SHUN CHEONG HONG)

Allen lock: QUALIFIED for **sales + cost of sales** because figures based on bank statement only.

天書 QO-C uses **revenue** / **cost of services** (not the words sales/COGS/營業額/銷貨成本). Closest locked template — use QO-C; do not invent a separate sales/COGS paragraph unless Allen amends 天書.

## S.407 paragraph (required)

```
REPORT ON OTHER MATTERS UNDER SECTIONS 407(2), 407(3) OF THE HONG KONG COMPANIES ORDINANCE

In respect alone of the inability to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the matters as described in the Basis for Qualified Opinion section above:

- we were unable to determine whether adequate accounting records had been kept; and
- we have not obtained all the information or explanations that, to the best of our knowledge and belief, are necessary and material for the purpose of the audit.
```

## Not QO-A records (minimal ops)

QO-A records limitation is framed as “minimal business operations with limited transactions” — **not** a good exact fit for ACTIVE trading. Prefer QO-C for bank-statement-based records.
