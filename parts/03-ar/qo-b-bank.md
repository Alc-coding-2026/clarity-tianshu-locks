## EN

```
BASIS FOR QUALIFIED OPINION

We were unable to obtain direct confirmation from the bank regarding the Company's bank balances and related banking arrangements as at [YE DATE]. Consequently, we were unable to determine whether any adjustments might be necessary in respect of bank balances, bank borrowings and contingent liabilities as disclosed in the financial statements.
```

Always retain all three: **bank balances, bank borrowings and contingent liabilities**, even if RL confirms nil borrowings.
