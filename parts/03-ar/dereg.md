## Period wording

EN: Replace "for the year then ended" with: "…income statement and statement of changes in equity for the period from [INCORP DATE] (date of incorporation) to [CESSATION DATE] (date of cessation of business)…"

中文: 將「截至該日止年度」改為: 「…由[成立日期]（成立日期）至[結業日期]（結業日期）期間之損益表及權益變動表…」

Mid-life: use **actual period start**, not incorporation, unless facts are first-period from incorp.

## Break-up EOM / 變現基礎強調事項

When the FS are prepared on a **break-up** basis (cessation + intended Cap.622 s.750 deregistration), the AR **requires** the locked Emphasis of Matter — see [`eom-breakup-basis.md`](./eom-breakup-basis.md) (EN+ZH).

- Disclosure of the break-up basis itself remains in **Note 2** (+ SoFP face caption): [`../05-notes/illustrative-dereg-breakup-basis.md`](../05-notes/illustrative-dereg-breakup-basis.md).
- The EOM **draws attention to Note 2** only.
- **Do not** write that the FS are prepared on a break-up basis *rather than* a going-concern basis / 而非持續經營基礎. Prefer **zero** “going concern” / 「持續經營」 words in this EOM (Allen hard rule).
