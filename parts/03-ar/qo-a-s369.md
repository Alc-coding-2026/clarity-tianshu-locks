# AR-QO-A — Period >18m / records (S.369(6))

Live: https://siumon.eternalorigins.net/tianshu/03-ar/qo-a-s369/

**Use when:** First FS period >18 months from incorporation, OR accounting records incomplete, OR both.

## S.407 rule (locked from 天書)

| Situation | S.407(2)/(3) Other Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Period >18m only (S.369(6)) | **No** |
| Records/completeness limitation | **Yes** (required) |
| Both | **Yes** (keep S.407) |
| Pure FEUPYC D-1 | **No** (see qo-d-feupyc) |
| Mid-life catch-up QUALIFIED (Allen lock) | **Yes** — see findings/midlife-catchup-includes-s407.md |

Sub-variants: **A-2** period only → delete records bullet + S.407. **A-3** records only → delete S.369(6); keep records + S.407.

## S.369(6) basis (EN, as shown)

Section 369(6) of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance requires that the accounting reference period of the first set of statutory audited financial statements to be within 18 months of incorporation.

The financial statements of the Company for the current period covering a period over 18 months have not been prepared in compliance with Section 369(6) of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance.

## Records limitation (EN, as shown)

Limitation of scope regarding maintenance of accounting records

- During the period under review, the Company conducted minimal business operations with limited transactions. While formal management accounts and a complete general ledger were not maintained throughout the period, the Company has prepared detailed profit and loss statements and financial statements based on available source documents. We have performed alternative audit procedures on these financial statements and supporting documentation. Although these procedures provided a reasonable basis for our opinion on the financial statements as presented, we were unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the completeness of all transactions throughout the period as would be available from contemporaneous accounting records maintained in accordance with section 373 of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance.

**Note:** This 天書 paragraph is framed as **minimal operations**. ACTIVE trading + sales/COGS from bank statements only is **not** confirmed as the same template until extracted (SCHL lookup).

## S.407 paragraph (required when records limitation present)

Source: `#s407-paragraph-required-when-records-limitation-present`

```
REPORT ON OTHER MATTERS UNDER SECTIONS 407(2), 407(3) OF THE HONG KONG COMPANIES ORDINANCE

In respect alone of the inability to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the matters as described in the Basis for Qualified Opinion section above:

- we were unable to determine whether adequate accounting records had been kept; and
- we have not obtained all the information or explanations that, to the best of our knowledge and belief, are necessary and material for the purpose of the audit.
```
